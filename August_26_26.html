Florida & National News Highlights | September 2026
Hurricane Season Enters Its Peak Period
September is historically the peak of the Atlantic hurricane season, making preparedness especially important for Florida residents. Families and businesses are encouraged to review emergency plans, know their evacuation zones, maintain disaster supply kits, and stay informed through official weather and emergency-management updates. Florida has continued emphasizing preparedness ahead of the 2026 hurricane season.
 
September Is Florida Preparedness Month
Florida recognizes September as Florida Preparedness Month, encouraging residents to take proactive steps before severe weather or other emergencies occur. It's a good time to update emergency contacts, review insurance information, protect important documents, and make sure household supplies are ready.
 
Back-to-School Season Brings Increased Traffic
With students returning to classrooms, Florida communities are adjusting to busier morning and afternoon commutes. Drivers should slow down in school zones, watch carefully for pedestrians and school buses, and allow additional travel time.
Labor Day Marks the Start of September
Labor Day falls on Monday, September 7, 2026, providing families across the country with a long weekend to travel, gather with friends, or enjoy the final days of summer. Labor Day is observed as a federal holiday on the first Monday of September.
 
National Preparedness Month
September also serves as National Preparedness Month, making it an ideal opportunity for households to think beyond everyday routines and prepare for emergencies. Reviewing communication plans, emergency supplies, important documents, and evacuation options can help families respond more effectively when unexpected situations arise.
 
Staying Alert to Online Scams
As consumers increasingly rely on digital services for shopping, banking, travel, and communication, staying alert to phishing emails, fraudulent text messages, and impersonation scams remains important. Consumers should verify unexpected requests for money or personal information and use multi-factor authentication whenever possible.
September Reminder
Preparedness starts before an emergency happens. Whether you're preparing for hurricane season, returning to school-year routines, or simply getting organized for fall, a few proactive steps today can make a meaningful difference tomorrow.

🍽️ Recipe of the Month | September 2026
🍎 Apple Cinnamon Baked Oatmeal
Welcome the beginning of fall with a warm, comforting breakfast featuring fresh apples, cinnamon, and hearty oats. This easy baked oatmeal is perfect for busy weekday mornings or a relaxed weekend brunch.
Ingredients:
• 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
• 1 tsp ground cinnamon
• ½ tsp baking powder
• ¼ tsp salt
• 2 eggs
• 1½ cups milk
• ¼ cup maple syrup
• 2 tbsp melted butter
• 1 tsp vanilla extract
• 2 medium apples, peeled and diced
• ¼ cup chopped walnuts or pecans (optional)
Directions:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and lightly grease an 8×8-inch baking dish.
2. In a bowl, combine oats, cinnamon, baking powder, and salt.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, maple syrup, melted butter, and vanilla.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the oat mixture and stir until combined.
5. Fold in the diced apples and nuts, then transfer everything to the baking dish.
6. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until the center is set and the top is lightly golden.
7. Let cool for a few minutes before serving.
Kitchen Tip:
Top each serving with a spoonful of Greek yogurt, a drizzle of maple syrup, or a sprinkle of cinnamon for an extra touch of fall flavor.
Warm, simple, and satisfying—this Apple Cinnamon Baked Oatmeal is a delicious way to welcome September!
 
🍹 Cocktail of the Month | September 2026
Apple Cider Bourbon Fizz
Bring a little fall flavor to your September gatherings with this refreshing Apple Cider Bourbon Fizz. Crisp apple cider, bourbon, fresh lemon, and sparkling water create a balanced cocktail that's perfect for transitioning from summer into autumn.
Ingredients:
• 2 oz bourbon
• 2 oz fresh apple cider
• ½ oz fresh lemon juice
• ½ oz maple syrup
• 2 oz sparkling water
• Ice
• Apple slice and cinnamon stick for garnish
Directions:
1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Add bourbon, apple cider, lemon juice, and maple syrup.
3. Shake well until chilled.
4. Strain into an ice-filled rocks glass.
5. Top with sparkling water and gently stir.
6. Garnish with an apple slice and cinnamon stick.
Mocktail Option
Skip the bourbon and combine apple cider, lemon juice, maple syrup, and sparkling water over ice for a refreshing alcohol-free version.
Cheers to September!
Crisp, lightly sweet, and refreshing, the Apple Cider Bourbon Fizz is an easy way to welcome the first flavors of fall while enjoying the last warm evenings of the season.
