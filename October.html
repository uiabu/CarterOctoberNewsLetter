🍽️ Recipe of the Month | October 2026
🎃 Creamy Pumpkin Soup
Warm, creamy, and packed with autumn flavor, this Pumpkin Soup is a comforting choice for October. It's easy to prepare and perfect for a cozy family dinner or a seasonal gathering.
Ingredients:
• 4 cups pumpkin purée
• 1 small yellow onion, diced
• 2 garlic cloves, minced
• 3 cups vegetable or chicken broth
• ½ cup heavy cream
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• ½ tsp ground cinnamon
• ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
• Salt and black pepper to taste
• Pumpkin seeds, for garnish
• Fresh parsley or thyme, for garnish
Directions:
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4–5 minutes until softened.
2. Add the garlic, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Stir in the pumpkin purée and broth. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 15 minutes.
4. Blend the soup with an immersion blender until smooth and creamy. Alternatively, carefully transfer it to a blender in batches.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and season with salt and black pepper.
6. Warm for another 2–3 minutes without bringing it to a boil.
7. Serve warm and garnish with pumpkin seeds and fresh herbs.
Kitchen Tip:
For extra richness, drizzle a little olive oil or cream over each bowl before serving. Pair it with crusty bread or a simple salad for a complete autumn meal.
Warm, creamy, and wonderfully seasonal, this Pumpkin Soup is the perfect taste of October!
 
🍹 Cocktail of the Month | October 2026
🎃 Spiced Apple Cider Mule
Celebrate October with a festive twist on the classic Moscow Mule. Crisp apple cider, ginger beer, fresh lime, and warming spices create a refreshing cocktail that's perfect for fall gatherings.
Ingredients:
• 2 oz vodka
• 3 oz fresh apple cider
• ½ oz fresh lime juice
• 3 oz ginger beer
• ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
• Pinch of ground nutmeg
• Ice
• Apple slice and cinnamon stick for garnish
Directions:
1. Fill a copper mug or rocks glass with ice.
2. Add vodka, apple cider, lime juice, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
3. Stir gently to combine.
4. Top with ginger beer and give it one gentle stir.
5. Garnish with an apple slice and cinnamon stick.
Mocktail Option
Skip the vodka and add extra ginger beer or sparkling water for a refreshing alcohol-free version.
Cheers to October!
Crisp apple cider, warming spices, and refreshing ginger make this Spiced Apple Cider Mule a festive choice for autumn dinners, Halloween gatherings, and cozy evenings with friends.
