Halloween Safety Tips

  • Slow down in neighborhoods. Expect more pedestrians, especially children, around homes, sidewalks, and crosswalks.
  • Watch for trick-or-treaters. Children may cross the street unexpectedly or appear between parked cars.
  • Be extra cautious at dusk and after dark. Reduce your speed and use your headlights to improve visibility.
  • Watch for costumes and decorations. Masks, dark clothing, and decorations can make pedestrians harder to see.
  • Stay alert in driveways and parking areas. Check carefully for children before pulling in or backing out.
