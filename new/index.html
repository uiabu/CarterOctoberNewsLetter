Why This 5K Matters

A Mission Worth Running For

Mary Lee's House works alongside community partners to provide a safe, respectful, and healing environment for children impacted by abuse and neglect.

Your participation in the Step Up Against Child Abuse 5K directly supports that mission. When you register, you help raise critical funds and awareness right here in Tampa.
