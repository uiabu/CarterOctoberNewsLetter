Our Purpose
Why We Step Up

Carter Injury Law is proud to partner with Mary Lee's House in recognition of Child Abuse Prevention Month this April. This family-friendly event is dedicated to raising awareness and critical funds to support children and families impacted by abuse and neglect.

"Mary Lee's House works to provide a safe, respectful, and healing environment for children during some of their most difficult moments."

All proceeds benefit Mary Lee's House directly. When you register, you are not just signing up for a race — you are helping create protection, advocacy, and healing opportunities where they are needed most.
