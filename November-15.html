Bite and Sip Spotlight

Gasparilla Distillery &
Cocktail Bar

Fall has arrived at Gasparilla Distillery & Cocktail Bar in Tampa's historic Ybor City! Known for its locally crafted rum, the distillery celebrates the season with cozy fall flavors, including pumpkin spiced rum, apple spiced rum, and a limited-time menu of seasonal cocktails. It's the perfect spot to sip something festive and embrace the autumn spirit.

 

Dockside Dave's

Located in Madeira Beach, Dockside Dave's is a longtime local favorite known for its famous grouper sandwiches, fresh seafood, and laid-back beach atmosphere. From crispy onion rings to coastal comfort food, it's the perfect spot to enjoy delicious food and soak up the relaxed Florida beach vibes.

 

Bandit Coffee Co.

Located in the heart of St. Petersburg's Grand Central District, Bandit Coffee Co. is a locally owned café and coffee roaster that brings a welcoming neighborhood atmosphere. Known for its expertly crafted espresso drinks, specialty coffee blends, and delicious scratch-made pastries, Bandit offers the perfect spot to start your morning or enjoy a relaxing coffee break.

Thanksgiving Food & Turkey
Giveaways Around Tampa Bay

With Thanksgiving just around the corner, several Tampa Bay organizations are hosting food and turkey giveaways to help local families enjoy a holiday meal. These community events offer opportunities for families to receive groceries, Thanksgiving essentials, and turkeys while spreading holiday cheer. Here are a few upcoming events to keep in mind:

  • The Grateful Table Thanksgiving Food Giveaway – November 14 at 11 a.m. at Mount Calvary Deliverance Church, 1509 E. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., Tampa. Registration is open, and food will be distributed while supplies last.
  • Boxed with Purpose Food Giveaway – November 16 from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at The Skills Center, 5107 N. 22nd Street, Tampa. Registered families can receive grocery packages with food staples. Registration is required.
  • HOPE813 Feed Tampa Bay Thanksgiving Outreach – November 21. The organization aims to provide 500 families with complete Thanksgiving meals, including turkeys and traditional sides. Families can register online.
