🍽️ Recipe of the Month | August 2026
Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta
Fresh, flavorful, and ready in under 30 minutes, this Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta is the perfect weeknight meal. It's light enough for warm August evenings while still being satisfying enough for the whole family.
Ingredients:
• 8 oz linguine or spaghetti
• 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
• 3 tbsp unsalted butter
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 4 garlic cloves, minced
• Juice of 1 lemon
• ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
• ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
• 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
• Salt and black pepper to taste
Directions:
1. Cook the pasta according to package directions. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water before draining.
2. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil and 1 tablespoon of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the shrimp for 1–2 minutes per side until pink, then remove from the skillet.
3. Add the remaining butter, garlic, and red pepper flakes to the skillet. Cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Stir in the lemon juice and a splash of the reserved pasta water.
5. Return the shrimp to the skillet, add the cooked pasta, and toss everything together until well coated.
6. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley before serving.
Kitchen Tip:
Pair this dish with a crisp garden salad and warm garlic bread for an easy summer dinner that's perfect for family meals or entertaining guests.
Simple, fresh, and packed with flavor—this Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta is a delicious way to enjoy the last weeks of summer!
 
🍹 Cocktail of the Month | August 2026
Peach Bourbon Smash
Celebrate the flavors of late summer with a refreshing Peach Bourbon Smash. Sweet ripe peaches, fresh mint, and smooth bourbon come together for a cocktail that's perfect for relaxing on the patio or entertaining friends.
Ingredients:
• 2 oz bourbon
• ½ ripe peach, sliced
• 6–8 fresh mint leaves
• ½ oz fresh lemon juice
• ½ oz simple syrup (adjust to taste)
• Crushed ice
• Peach slice and mint sprig for garnish
Directions:
1. In a cocktail shaker, muddle the peach slices, mint leaves, lemon juice, and simple syrup.

2. Add the bourbon and a handful of ice.

3. Shake well until chilled.

4. Strain into a rocks glass filled with crushed ice.

5. Garnish with a fresh peach slice and a sprig of mint.
Mocktail Option
Replace the bourbon with sparkling peach tea or ginger ale for a refreshing alcohol-free version that's equally delicious.
Cheers to August!
Whether you're enjoying a backyard barbecue, a sunset on the porch, or a weekend gathering with friends, the Peach Bourbon Smash is a refreshing way to savor the final weeks of summer.
