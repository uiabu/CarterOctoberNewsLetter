🍽️ Recipe of the Month – June 2026

Summer Mango Shrimp Tacos

A fresh and flavorful summer recipe perfect for warm June evenings. These mango shrimp tacos combine juicy grilled shrimp, sweet mango salsa, and crisp vegetables for a light meal ideal for family dinners, cookouts, or weekend gatherings.

🛒 Ingredients (Serves 4)

  • 1 pound shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 8 small flour or corn tortillas
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1/4 red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish
  • 1/2 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare the shrimp
    • In a bowl, toss shrimp with olive oil, chili powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and half the lime juice.
  2. Make the mango salsa
    • Combine diced mango, red onion, cilantro, and remaining lime juice in a separate bowl. Mix well and chill until ready to serve.
  3. Cook the shrimp
    • Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat. Cook shrimp for 2–3 minutes per side until pink and fully cooked.
  4. Warm the tortillas
    • Lightly heat tortillas on the grill or skillet for about 30 seconds per side.
  5. Assemble the tacos
    • Fill tortillas with shredded cabbage, shrimp, mango salsa, and avocado slices.
  6. Add finishing touches
    • Top with sour cream or Greek yogurt and garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.

💡 Tips

  • Serve with tortilla chips and guacamole for a complete summer meal.
  • Add jalapeños or hot sauce for extra spice.
  • Substitute shrimp with grilled chicken or tofu for different variations.
  • Chill the mango salsa before serving for even fresher flavor.

🍹 Cocktail of the Month – June 2026

Citrus Paloma Spritz

A bright, refreshing cocktail that’s perfect for warm May days. With fresh citrus and a sparkling finish, this drink is light, crisp, and ideal for outdoor gatherings.

🛒 Ingredients (Serves 2)

  • 3 oz tequila
  • 2 oz fresh grapefruit juice
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup (or to taste)
  • Club soda or sparkling water
  • Ice cubes
  • Grapefruit slice & lime wedge (for garnish)
  • Salt or sugar (optional, for rim)

🍸 Instructions

  1. Prepare the glass
    • (Optional) Rim glasses with salt or sugar by rubbing a lime wedge around the edge and dipping into salt or sugar.
  2. Mix the base
    • In a shaker, combine tequila, grapefruit juice, lime juice, and simple syrup with ice.
    • Shake well.
  3. Assemble
    • Fill glasses with fresh ice.
    • Strain the mixture into each glass.
  4. Top and garnish
    • Add a splash of club soda and stir gently.
    • Garnish with a grapefruit slice and lime wedge.

💡 Tips

  • Adjust sweetness by adding more or less simple syrup.
  • Use fresh-squeezed citrus for the best flavor.
  • For a lighter version, add extra sparkling water.
