Bite and Sip Spotlight

Mise en Place

Mise en Place is a Tampa favorite offering upscale New American cuisine with creative, seasonal dishes. Known for thoughtful combinations like roasted butternut squash with blue cheese, pickled apples, pancetta, pistachios, and fried sage, the menu highlights fresh flavors with an autumn-inspired twist. Featuring a sophisticated atmosphere and innovative menu, this is a great spot for a special dinner or date night.

 

Dirty Laundry Speakeasy

Dirty Laundry Speakeasy is a hidden St. Petersburg speakeasy that went viral on TikTok for its secret entrance, vintage-inspired atmosphere, and unique experience. Tucked behind a laundromat-style storefront, this popular spot serves creative craft cocktails alongside small bites and live entertainment.

 

Kahwa Coffee

Kahwa Coffee is a Tampa favorite known for locally roasted coffee, creative drinks, and seasonal flavors. This fall, coffee lovers can enjoy the return of its Pumpkin Spice Latte, made with espresso, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and creamy milk. Whether you prefer it hot or iced, Kahwa's seasonal menu is a perfect way to welcome the flavors of fall while enjoying a locally loved Tampa coffee shop.

Back-to-School Safety Tips

  • Slow down in school zones. Give yourself extra time during morning drop-off and afternoon pickup.
  • Watch for pedestrians. Children may cross unexpectedly, especially near schools, bus stops, and crosswalks.
  • Give school buses plenty of space. Stop when required and never pass a stopped bus with its lights flashing.
  • Watch for bikes and scooters. Be especially alert around neighborhoods and school entrances.
  • Plan for rain. September showers can make roads slippery and visibility poor, so slow down and leave extra following distance.

Supporting Tampa Students

Back-to-school season isn't just about new backpacks and fresh notebooks. Through the Feeding Minds School Pantry Program, families at Hillsborough County schools receive free, nutritious groceries. Supporting students means supporting the whole family.
