🍽️ Recipe of the Month | November 2026
🦃 Classic Herb-Roasted Turkey Breast
Celebrate the flavors of fall with a juicy, herb-roasted turkey breast. Seasoned with garlic, rosemary, thyme, and butter, this comforting dish is perfect for Thanksgiving dinner or a cozy family gathering.
Ingredients:
• 3–4 lb bone-in or boneless turkey breast
• 4 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
• 3 garlic cloves, minced
• 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
• 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
• 1 tsp paprika
• 1 tbsp olive oil
• 1 tsp salt
• ½ tsp black pepper
• 1 lemon, cut into wedges
• ½ cup chicken broth
Directions:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Pat the turkey breast dry with paper towels.
2. In a small bowl, mix the softened butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
3. Gently rub the herb butter over the turkey breast. Drizzle with olive oil.
4. Place the turkey in a roasting pan and arrange the lemon wedges around it. Pour the chicken broth into the bottom of the pan.
5. Roast for approximately 1½–2 hours, depending on the size and whether the breast is bone-in or boneless. Check the internal temperature with a food thermometer.
6. Remove the turkey when the thickest part reaches 165°F. Let it rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing.
7. Serve with roasted vegetables, mashed potatoes, or your favorite Thanksgiving sides.
Kitchen Tip:
For extra flavor, baste the turkey occasionally with the pan juices. Always use a food thermometer rather than relying on cooking time or color alone.
🍂 A Taste of Thanksgiving
Tender, flavorful, and infused with fragrant herbs, this roasted turkey breast is a delicious centerpiece for your November table. Gather your loved ones and enjoy a comforting meal filled with the flavors of the season!
 
🍹 Cocktail of the Month | November 2026
🍎 Cranberry Apple Cider Margarita
Celebrate the flavors of fall with a festive Cranberry Apple Cider Margarita. Tart cranberry, crisp apple cider, fresh lime, and smooth tequila create a colorful seasonal cocktail that's perfect for Thanksgiving gatherings and cozy autumn evenings.
Ingredients:
• 2 oz silver tequila
• 2 oz apple cider
• 1 oz cranberry juice
• ½ oz fresh lime juice
• ½ oz orange liqueur
• ½ oz simple syrup, optional
• Ice
• Cinnamon sugar and a lime wedge for the rim
• Fresh cranberries and an apple slice for garnish
Directions:
1. Run a lime wedge around the rim of a margarita glass, then dip the rim into cinnamon sugar.

2. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.

3. Add the tequila, apple cider, cranberry juice, lime juice, orange liqueur, and simple syrup if desired.

4. Shake well for 15–20 seconds until thoroughly chilled.

5. Fill the prepared glass with fresh ice and strain the cocktail into it.

6. Garnish with fresh cranberries and an apple slice.
Mocktail Option
Skip the tequila and orange liqueur. Combine apple cider, cranberry juice, fresh lime juice, and a splash of sparkling water over ice for a refreshing alcohol-free alternative.
🍂 Cheers to November!
With its festive cranberry color and warm apple-cinnamon notes, this Cranberry Apple Cider Margarita is a delicious way to celebrate the season. Serve it at Thanksgiving dinner, a holiday gathering, or a relaxed evening with friends.
